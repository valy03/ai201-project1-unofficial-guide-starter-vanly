UCSC Dining locations.

Dining halls: College Nine/John R. Lewis (C9/JRL), Cowell/Stevenson, Crown/Merrill, Porter/Kresge, Rachel Carson/Oakes.
Cafes and restaurants: Banana Joe's Late Night, Global Village Cafe, Oakes Cafe, Owl's Nest, Stevenson Coffee House, University Center Cafe, University Center Bistro (closed until Fall 2026).
Perk Coffee Bars: Baskin Engineering, Earth & Marine Sciences, Physical Sciences Building.
Markets: Merrill Market, Porter Market, Slug Stop (closed for construction until further notice).

Regular weekly serving hours for each dining hall:

College Nine/John R. Lewis Dining Hall (C9/JRL) regular hours:
Tuesday through Saturday: Breakfast 7-11AM, Continuous Dining 11-11:30AM, Lunch 11:30AM-2PM, Continuous Dining 2-5PM, Dinner 5-8PM, Late Night 8-11PM.
Sunday and Monday: Breakfast 7-11AM, Continuous Dining 11-11:30AM, Lunch 11:30AM-2PM, Continuous Dining 2-5PM, Dinner 5-8PM (no Late Night).
So College Nine/John R. Lewis is open 7AM to 11PM Tuesday-Saturday and 7AM to 8PM Sunday-Monday. It offers Late Night service, which most dining halls do not.

Cowell/Stevenson Dining Hall regular hours:
Monday through Thursday: Breakfast 7-11AM, Continuous Dining 11-11:30AM, Lunch 11:30AM-2PM, Continuous Dining 2-5PM, Dinner 5-8PM, Late Night 8-11PM.
Friday: Breakfast 7-11AM, Continuous Dining 11-11:30AM, Lunch 11:30AM-2PM, Continuous Dining 2-5PM, Dinner 5-8PM (no Late Night).
Saturday: Breakfast 7-10AM, Brunch 10AM-2PM, Continuous Dining 2-5PM, Dinner 5-8PM.
Sunday: Breakfast 7-10AM, Brunch 10AM-2PM, Continuous Dining 2-5PM, Dinner 5-8PM, Late Night 8-11PM.

Crown/Merrill Dining Hall regular hours:
Monday through Friday: Breakfast 7-11AM, Continuous Dining 11-11:30AM (limited entree options), Lunch 11:30AM-2PM, Continuous Dining 2-5PM (limited entree options), Dinner 5-8PM.
Crown/Merrill Dining Hall is closed on weekends.
