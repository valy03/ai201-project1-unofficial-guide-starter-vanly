Best restaurants in Santa Cruz (near UCSC)

Gabriella Cafe: Italian restaurant. A charming and intimate spot located in downtown Santa Cruz.

La Posta Restaurant: Italian restaurant. Features super-thin pizzas and homemade bread from the wood-fired oven.

Crow's Nest Restaurant: American and seafood. Located at the mouth of the Santa Cruz Harbor with bay views.

The Buttery: Bakery, breakfast, and cafe. A bustling bakery known for its breads, pastries, and custom cakes.

Laili Restaurant: Mediterranean and Afghan. Offers everything from traditional kebabs to aromatic rice dishes.

Jack O'Neill Restaurant & Lounge: Seafood and California cuisine. A retro-chic dining spot near the boardwalk.

Las Palmas Taco Bar: Mexican. A cozy, family-owned spot offering a menu of Mexican classics.

Oswald Restaurant: American and Californian. A chic and elegant dining spot featuring creative American dishes.

Jack's Hamburgers: Hamburger restaurant. A fast-food spot that offers classic American fare.

Pearl of the Ocean: Sri Lankan, Asian, organic (noted as permanently closed).

Ideal Bar & Grill: Bar and grill, American. Located at the entrance of the Santa Cruz Wharf.

Stagnaro Bros. Seafood: Seafood. Known for classic dishes like clam chowder and fish and chips.

Riva Fish House: Seafood and American. A classic Santa Cruz restaurant located on the wharf.
