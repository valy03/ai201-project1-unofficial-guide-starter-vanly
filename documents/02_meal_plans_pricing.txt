Residence Hall Meal Plans and Pricing

Unlimited Meal Plans

7-Day Unlimited Plan: $2406 per quarter. Unlimited dining hall entry 7 days a week. Includes 75 Slug Points per quarter.

5-Day Unlimited Plan: $1788 per quarter. Unlimited dining hall entry Monday through Friday only. Includes 75 Slug Points per quarter.

Slug Points Meal Plans

Blue Slug Points Plan (the minimum required plan): $1359 per quarter, which is 1359 Slug Points per quarter, about $124 average weekly spending.

Gold Slug Points Plan: $1905 per quarter, which is 1905 Slug Points per quarter, about $173 average weekly spending.

Banana Slug Points Plan: $2592 per quarter, which is 2592 Slug Points per quarter, about $236 average weekly spending.

Meal Plan Currency Rules

Slug Points: Slug Points reset at the beginning of each quarter. They do not carry over into the following quarter, nor are they reimbursed.

Banana Bucks: Banana Bucks carry over quarter to quarter and expire at the end of the academic year in June (June 15, 2026). Banana Bucks can be added anytime, but cannot be used until you have used up all of the Slug Points in your Residence Hall meal plan for the quarter.

Flexi Dollars: Similar to Banana Bucks, Flexi Dollars carry over from quarter to quarter and expire at the end of the academic year in June.

All currencies (Slug Points, Banana Bucks, and Flexi Dollars) provide a 10% discount on orders from cafes, markets, and coffee bars.
